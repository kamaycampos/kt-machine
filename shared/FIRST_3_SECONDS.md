# THE FIRST 3 SECONDS

Kamay, 30 Sept 2026:

> "what really determines if a clip goes viral it really is the FIRST 3 SECONDS, THATS ALL THAT
> MATTERS... if the first 3 seconds has the most impactful hook affecting the viewer in the most of
> their senses possible in all ways possible, they will stay, they will get hypnotized."

He is right, and our own numbers say it in a colder way: the clips in the top decile hold people
(skip rate **36.8%**, watch **39.4s**); the rest lose half the audience immediately (**47.8%**,
**22.9s**). Same account, same man, same lighting. **The difference is the opening.**

This file governs the opening. Everything else in the pipeline serves it.

## The law
Three seconds decide the clip. They must hit **more than one sense at once**: something to read,
something to hear, something that moves. A clip that opens with a static line of white text on a
frame of a man talking is asking the viewer to volunteer attention. Nobody volunteers.

## What the first 3 seconds must carry
1. **A sentence that cannot be scrolled past.** The most arresting line in the whole clip belongs at
   second zero - not the start of the story, the sharpest moment of it. If the best line is at 0:40,
   the clip starts at 0:40 and the story fills in after.
2. **A hook card that belongs to THIS clip.** Colour taken from the clip's own first frame - its
   accent from the strongest colour in the picture, never a house colour dropped on top. Kamay's
   reference: "the captions are congruent and in alignment colour and cinematics... every clip has
   different edits because it's in accordance with the nature of the visual clip."
3. **Movement.** A slow push-in across the three seconds, or the words arriving one beat after
   another. Stillness reads as a still image and a still image gets scrolled.
4. **Sound.** A short sting under the hook - a low hit or a rising sweep, mixed under his voice, not
   over it. This is the sense we have never used at all, and it is the one every viral account uses.
5. **The promise paid.** Whatever the three seconds claim, the clip delivers - see HOOK_PATTERNS.md.
   A hook that lies buys one view and costs the account.

## What decides which moment gets cut
Not the topic. Kamay, 30 Sept: *"basically all of KT videos have value... it's not much about the
type of video like houses, yes it is but not much. What it really is about is the first 3 sec hooks
and the quality of the video. Think about people's emotions and what their needs are and how to
influence them positively."*

So the cut is chosen by **the strength of its opening moment and the emotion underneath it**:
fear of being left behind, the shame of being broke, wanting to be the person who knows, wanting to
protect a family, wanting to be free of a job. Pick the moment where Kevin says something that lands
on one of those, and open exactly there.

House and property clips still carry a real edge in the numbers (every breakout this account has had
is one), so prefer them **when the moment is equally strong** - never over a stronger opening.

## The scoreboard
**Skip rate, per clip, from Instagram insights.** It is the only number that measures the first
seconds directly. Median today: 47%. Target: under 40%. Every change to the opening is judged on it,
not on taste and not on likes.
