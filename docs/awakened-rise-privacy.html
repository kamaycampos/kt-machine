Privacy Policy - The Awakened Rise

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers the "Awakened Rise Replies" app, which The Awakened Rise uses on its own Instagram account and Facebook Page to answer people who ask for a link in a comment.

What we read

When you comment on a post by The Awakened Rise, the app reads the comment's text, its time and its ID, so it can tell whether you asked for the link (for example by commenting the word shown in the caption).

What we do with it

If your comment asks for the link, the app sends you one private message with that link and may post one public reply telling you to check your messages. It never messages you twice for the same comment and never messages anyone who did not comment.

What we keep

We keep only the comment's ID, the keyword, and the time the message was sent, so the same comment is never answered twice. We do not keep your name, profile, or the content of your messages, and we never sell or share any data.

Deleting your data

To have any record of your comment removed, send a message to The Awakened Rise on Instagram or Facebook asking for deletion. It will be removed within 30 days. Deleting your comment also ends any further processing of it.
