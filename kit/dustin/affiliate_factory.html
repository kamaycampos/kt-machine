A member is live in three steps

A member needs Claude Pro ($20 a month), a free GitHub account and their affiliate link. Nothing else to buy, install or host.

Copy the factory

One button, Use this template, makes their own copy of the whole machine.

Open it in Claude

At claude.ai/code they pick their copy. Claude already knows the whole system from the kit's instructions.

Type start

Claude asks six questions about their voice and audience, sets everything up and gives each click, one at a time.

From then on, the factory runs on five words: plan picks the week's clips in their voice, clips shows what was made, report says what is winning, post switches on autopilot, and anything else they ask for (a theme week, a series, a new angle) Claude turns into clips.

What the factory does by itself

Claude makes one judgment: which moments to clip. Everything else is automatic, and every clip passes a set of checks that exist because a real clip once failed them.

Stock

Pulls Kevin's episodes in HD and keeps them encrypted.gate: 1080p minimum source

Transcribe

Word-level transcript of every episode, 16 at a time in parallel.

Choose

The member's Claude reads every episode and picks 6-9 moments, led by the strongest opening line and the emotion under it.gates: six-word hook with a concrete number or name · no worn-out words · no repeated topics

Cut

Cuts on the exact first and last words, so a clip never opens mid-sentence or ends before the payoff.gates: named edges found in the audio · no advert or testimonial endings

Render

Full-screen 9:16 that follows Kevin's face, heavy capitals with one gold payoff line, captions of 1-3 words locked to the sound.gates: captions checked against the waveform · first and last second must be speech · 1080×1920

Schedule & post

Spreads clips across the member's own time slots and writes a caption for each platform's link rules.rules: 45 min apart · one source at most twice a day · never the same clip twice

Learn

Reads back views, skip rate, shares and saves, and keeps a living record of what works so every next week's choices get sharper.

Every member's factory is their own

Their brand file

Claude writes it with them on day one and reads it before every task. It holds:

  • their name and handles, and who they talk to
  • their lane, for example 50% wealth habits, 20% real estate, 30% Your Wish Is Your Command
  • their voice: teacher, challenger, storyteller or their own
  • their comment keyword and their link
  • their platforms and posting times

A proven starting point

Every member starts with what was learned on the pilot account:

  • method clips out-reach warnings, 30-40× on shares and saves
  • a "What…" opener beats "How…" on TikTok
  • full-screen beats letterbox by 20-100×
  • BRAIN draws 7× the comments FREE does

Their own numbers then refine it for their account.

Posting: studio on day one, autopilot when ready

Compliance is built in

The kit's rules are enforced on every plan, because one strike costs a member more than any clip earns:

  • only footage the affiliate program authorises
  • an affiliate disclosure on every post
  • no health or cure claims
  • no income promises
  • no words Kevin did not say
  • members post as themselves, never as Kevin or GIN

Four decisions from your team

  1. Authorised footage. Which sources may affiliates clip: Kevin's public episodes, the Affiliate Portal library, or both? We also need a way for GIN's copyright team to recognise a member's clips, so it does not take them down.
  2. Disclosure wording. The exact affiliate disclosure the program wants on every post.
  3. Links. How each member's tracked link is built, so the kit can check that it is set up correctly.
  4. Five pilot members. Names, and a start date.
