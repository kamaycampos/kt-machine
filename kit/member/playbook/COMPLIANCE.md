# COMPLIANCE: what keeps your account, your commissions and the program safe

Your Claude checks every plan against this page. These rules protect you. A platform strike or a takedown costs you more than any single clip ever earns.

## 1. Use only footage you are authorised to use
GIN actively protects Kevin's content: its copyright team files takedowns against unauthorised uploads. Clip only the sources the affiliate program approves for affiliates. **The approved source list comes from the affiliate program; ask them if you are unsure.** Never clip paid courses, processes, private events or member-only material.

## 2. Always disclose
Every caption carries a plain affiliate disclosure, for example *"I earn a commission if you buy through my link."* That is the law (the FTC in the US, and similar rules elsewhere), and it builds trust.

## 3. No health or cure claims
Do not post clips or captions that promise a treatment, cure or medical result. The planner already skips health and diet advice. Keep it that way.

## 4. No income promises
Never say or imply that a viewer will earn a specific amount. "Here is how wealthy people think about money" is fine. "You will make $10,000" is not.

## 5. Never put words in Kevin's mouth
The hook and the caption must match what he actually says in the clip. Never fake a quote, an endorsement or a result.

## 6. You are you, not Kevin
Never present your account as Kevin's or as GIN's official account. Use your own name and handles, and write "Kevin Trudeau" in full in each caption, as the person you are learning from.

## 7. Skip these topics
Politics, immigration, religion arguments, specific living private people, and sales pitches for paid programs or processes.
