# The Affiliate Factory

**Your own clip studio for Kevin Trudeau's content. You choose the message, and Claude and this machine make the clips.**

It finds the strongest moments in Kevin's episodes and cuts them to full-screen 9:16. It burns in a six-word hook and captions timed to every word, writes the post caption, and schedules everything. With autopilot on, it posts to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok by itself. It runs on GitHub for free. All you pay for is Claude Pro.

It was built and proven on a live account first:

- **214,805 views** on one reel
- **6 posts a day** to 4 platforms
- **$0** a month in servers

## What you need
- **Claude Pro** ($20/month): [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)
- A free **GitHub** account: [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup)
- Your **affiliate link** from the KT affiliate portal

## Three steps

**1. Make your copy.** At the top of this page, click **Use this template → Create a new repository**.
- Name it anything, for example `my-factory`.
- Keep it **Public**. That keeps GitHub free with no limits. Your videos are stored encrypted.

**2. Open it in Claude.** Go to [claude.ai/code](https://claude.ai/code), connect GitHub when it asks, and pick your new repository.

**3. Type `start`.** Claude asks you six questions about your voice and your audience, sets up the machine, and tells you each click.

That's it. After that, these words run your factory:

| You type | What happens |
|---|---|
| `plan` | Claude reads new episodes and chooses this week's clips in your voice |
| `clips` | What got made, with captions ready to copy |
| `report` | What's winning, what's not, and what to change next week |
| `post` | Switch on autopilot, one platform at a time |
| `style` | Change your look: colours, font, capitals, shadow, timing |
| `teach` | Show it a reel you love, and your next clips learn from it |
| `update` | Get the newest kit version, and keep everything that's yours |
| anything else | Ask for a theme week, a series, a new style. It's your studio. |

## Your control panel
Your factory has its own website at `https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/`. It shows every clip, where it came from, how it's doing, what's next, your look and your voice. Everything on it is one tap from being changed, and it updates itself every 20 minutes.

## Two ways to run it
- **Studio mode** (day one): the machine makes and schedules your clips, and you post them from your board with one tap.
- **Autopilot** (when you're ready): connect your accounts with `post` and it publishes by itself, on your schedule, spaced like a human would post.
